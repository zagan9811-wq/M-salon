03 About

One barber,
one chair,
one price.

M Salon is run by Mohammed Qasim in Kadhimiya, Baghdad.

Every cut costs the same and every appointment is held for the same length of time. That is the whole system. It means the man in the chair gets the full slot, and the man who booked after him walks in at his own time instead of watching the clock from a bench.

Book the day and the hour that suit you, arrive a few minutes early, and leave sharp. If you need a time the calendar does not show, call the shop — the phone still works the old way.
